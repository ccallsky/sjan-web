About

Research should be rigorous enough for science and useful enough for the real world.

Research Philosophy

I am interested in research that connects statistical reasoning, machine learning, domain knowledge, and measurable real-world impact. Rather than treating AI as a black box, I prefer models that help us understand why systems behave the way they do.

Current Focus

My current work spans AI-assisted environmental modeling, time-series prediction, regional digital transformation, data-driven policy, and practical AI education.
